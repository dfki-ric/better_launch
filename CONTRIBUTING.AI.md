# AI-Assisted Contributions
I strongly believe that there is good cause for humans doing human things. We are already living in a time where millions of things demand our attention. And while it may seem like generative AIs are a solution to this, they are not. They are a tool to spread our attention even thinner, alienating us from the very things we used to enjoy.

Should you decide to make a PR using some form AI I have 3 fundamental expectations:

1) You clearly state what AI(s) you used and how much they contributed to your PR.
2) You understand the generated code and the problem it's supposed to solve.
3) You personally went through *ALL* generated code and cleaned it up.

All PRs will be reviewed by me in person, and no PR will be accepted unless you follow these rules. If during the review process I get the impression that I'm talking to an AI, I will immediately reject your PR.
